Quantum Rewards

Quantum Rewards is an Android rewards application by QuantumSoftwares. It brings a QPoints balance together with eligible in-app activities and partner offers, giving users one place to track rewards earned in the app.

Android QPoints rewards Partner offers Publisher: QuantumSoftwares

How rewards work

  1. Users open an available activity or partner offer in Quantum Rewards.
  2. They review the offer requirements and complete the advertiser's instructions.
  3. The partner verifies the completion and sends a transaction notification to our rewards service.
  4. Eligible QPoints are added to the user's in-app balance after a valid confirmation.

QPoints

QPoints are the virtual rewards units used inside Quantum Rewards. The app displays the user's balance and the in-app uses currently available to that account.

Reward amounts, eligibility, completion checks and approval times can vary by offer, advertiser, provider, device and country.

Offer verification and reward integrity

Offers are supplied and measured by independent advertising partners. QuantumSoftwares uses provider transaction notifications to reconcile completed offers and QPoints. Duplicate, invalid, reversed or unconfirmed transactions are not credited as successful completions.

Completing an offer does not guarantee an immediate reward. The provider and advertiser determine whether their completion conditions have been met.

Privacy and third-party services

When a user chooses to open a partner offerwall, the provider may process information needed to present, measure and validate offers. Depending on the provider and campaign, this can include a publisher user identifier, IP address, country, device and operating-system information, advertising identifiers where available, and offer interaction or completion events.

Providers process information under their own privacy terms. QuantumSoftwares receives the transaction information needed to attribute valid completions and maintain the QPoints ledger. Please review our Privacy Policy for the developer's broader privacy information.
